Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform
Ledgerbridge Financial - Remote (UK/EU)

About Ledgerbridge

Ledgerbridge Financial builds settlement infrastructure for mid-sized merchants across
Europe. We process a lot of money on behalf of businesses who need to trust that it
moves correctly, every time. Our platform team owns everything from card-network
integration to nightly settlement, and we're growing it.

The role

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Payments Platform team. You'll
work on the systems that move money between merchants, card networks, and banks, and
you'll be one of the people the rest of engineering leans on when a payment doesn't
reconcile. This is a hands-on engineering role, not a management track.

What you'll do

- Design and build services in the core settlement and reconciliation path
- Take ownership of at least one production service end to end
- Be part of the on-call rotation for payment-critical systems
- Work closely with the risk and compliance teams on how we handle sensitive data
- Help less experienced engineers on the team grow

What you'll need

- 5+ years building backend services in Go, Java, or a similar statically typed language
- Experience with Kafka or a comparable message broker in production
- Familiarity with PCI-DSS or a similar compliance framework, even if you weren't the
  one formally responsible for it
- On-call experience for production systems where money is on the line
- Experience mentoring other engineers
- A strong communicator who can explain a tricky bug to someone outside the team

Nice to have

- Prior experience at a fintech, payments, or banking company
- Rust experience
- Experience managing a team's infrastructure or cloud budget
- Conference speaking experience
- Comfortable working in a company with 200+ engineers

Benefits & perks

Competitive salary and equity, private health cover, 28 days holiday plus bank
holidays, a home office stipend, and a genuinely flexible remote-first culture.
Ledgerbridge Financial is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applicants
regardless of background.
