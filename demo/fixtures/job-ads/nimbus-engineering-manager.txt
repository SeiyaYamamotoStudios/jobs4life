Engineering Manager, Platform Team
Nimbus Cloudworks - Hybrid (Berlin) or Remote (EU)

Who we are

Nimbus Cloudworks makes the internal developer platform that other companies build
their own platforms on top of. Our Platform team is the backbone of the engineering
org: deployment tooling, on-call standards, and the infrastructure everyone else
depends on to ship.

The role

We're hiring an Engineering Manager to lead our Platform team through its next phase
of growth. You'll manage a small group of backend engineers, own the team's roadmap
and budget, and be the person who represents platform concerns when the rest of the
company is planning. We want someone who has been a hands-on engineer before and
still remembers what that's like.

What you'll own

- Directly managing a team of software engineers: hiring, growth, and performance
- The team's budget for cloud infrastructure spend
- Running incident response and postmortems when platform services go down
- Representing the team in quarterly cross-org planning
- Setting the team's technical roadmap in partnership with senior engineers

What we're looking for

- 3+ years directly managing a team of software engineers, including hiring and
  performance reviews
- Experience owning a team budget, not just contributing estimates to one
- A background as a hands-on software engineer before moving into management
- Experience running incident response and postmortems for production outages
- Comfortable representing the team in cross-org planning conversations
- Genuinely good at giving feedback that people can act on

Nice to have

- Experience in a regulated industry such as payments or healthcare
- Familiarity with Kubernetes or a comparable orchestration platform
- Experience scaling a team from a handful of engineers to a larger one
- Public speaking or conference experience
- Experience setting a company-wide technical strategy, not just a team one

What we offer

Competitive compensation, a relocation package if you need one, generous parental
leave, and a team that has been through an incident or two together. Nimbus
Cloudworks is committed to building a diverse and inclusive team.
