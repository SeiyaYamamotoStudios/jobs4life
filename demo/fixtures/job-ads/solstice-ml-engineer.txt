Machine Learning Engineer - Perception
Solstice Vision Labs - Onsite (Toronto) preferred, remote considered for senior candidates

About us

Solstice Vision Labs builds computer vision systems for autonomous inspection robots
used in warehouses and light manufacturing. Our perception stack has to work on
real, messy hardware in the field, not just in a notebook.

The role

We're looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our Perception team. You'll work
across the full lifecycle of our vision models: collecting and labeling data, training,
and getting models running reliably on the robot's onboard compute. This is a
production-focused role - we care more about a model that runs correctly on the robot
than one that looks good on a benchmark.

Responsibilities

- Build and improve computer vision models used in production on our robots
- Work with our data annotation pipeline to decide what gets labeled next
- Get models running within the memory and latency limits of embedded hardware
- Debug production failures back to a root cause, whether that's data, model, or camera
- Collaborate with hardware engineers on camera placement and calibration

What you'll need

- Hands-on experience building and deploying computer vision models to a production
  system, not only research or coursework
- Strong Python skills; C++ experience is a plus for the embedded-deployment side
- Experience with PyTorch or a comparable deep learning framework
- Experience working with a data annotation or labeling pipeline
- Comfortable working within hardware constraints such as edge or embedded deployment

Nice to have

- Peer-reviewed publications or patents related to computer vision or robotics
- Experience mentoring junior scientists or engineers
- Prior robotics industry experience
- Experience helping set technical research direction for a team, not just executing it
- Familiarity with backend or cloud infrastructure, for teams that also ship a
  cloud-connected fleet dashboard

Compensation & benefits

Salary band posted per local pay transparency requirements, equity, health benefits,
and relocation assistance for candidates moving to Toronto. Solstice Vision Labs is
an equal opportunity employer.
