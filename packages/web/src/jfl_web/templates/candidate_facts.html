What your CVs claim

A model read your CVs and proposed these facts. None of them counts as evidence yet. Anything you confirm here becomes what your future claims are measured against — in your words, stored exactly as you write them — so it is worth being slower here than anywhere else in this tool.

There is no "accept everything" button, on purpose. A CV is written to persuade, and confirming a whole stack of them in one click would quietly make every stretch in them into evidence under your name — which is the one thing this tool exists to stop. You can accept a single role's facts at a time, once you have read them.
