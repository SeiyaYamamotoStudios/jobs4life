Profile

What you will and will not take, what you can actually do, and what a move is for. Every field is optional — anything you skip stays not stated, never guessed at. Nothing on this page calls a model.

Four of the five sections are preferences: they decide whether a role suits you and are never treated as evidence about your career. The fifth, how you would put your own depth, is a claim about you — so it goes on the record alongside the facts you have confirmed, in your words, and can be cited as evidence. That section says so again where you type it.
